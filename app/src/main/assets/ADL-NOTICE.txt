android-debloat-list.zip is the unmodified upstream repository archive.
Cana reads aosp.json, carrier.json, google.json, misc.json, oem.json and
suggestions/*.json. The app maps delete/replace/caution/unsafe to its
Recommended/Advanced/Expert/Unsafe badges, preserving upstream warnings,
dependencies, references and replacement notes. No local audit or verdict
overrides are applied.
